COMPANY PROFILE

Who we are

Guizhou Guanshan Lake Middle East Contracting L.L.C. is a legally registered, licensed construction company in Dubai, approved by Dubai Economy & Tourism (DET). We maintain a long-term, stable partnership with CITIC Construction and are one of its important subcontracting partners in the UAE.

With standardized management, professional construction capability and a localized delivery team, we take on residential, commercial and mixed-use developments across the UAE.
